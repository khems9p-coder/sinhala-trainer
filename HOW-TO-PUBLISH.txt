SINHALA WORD TRAINER — HOW TO PUT IT ON THE GOOGLE PLAY STORE
Compiled by Khem Chetry
===============================================================

BEFORE YOU START
- Open privacy.html in any text editor and replace [YOUR EMAIL ADDRESS]
  with your email. Google requires a contact address.
- You will need: a Google (Gmail) account, a debit/credit card for the
  one-time US$25 fee (prepaid cards are not accepted), and a government ID.


STEP 1 — PUT THE APP ONLINE (free, with GitHub Pages)
1. Go to github.com and create a free account.
2. Click "+" (top right) > "New repository".
   Name it:  sinhala-trainer     Choose "Public". Click "Create repository".
3. Click "uploading an existing file". Drag in EVERYTHING from this folder
   (index.html, manifest.json, sw.js, privacy.html and the "icons" folder).
   Click "Commit changes".
4. Open the repository's "Settings" > "Pages".
   Under "Branch" choose "main" and "/ (root)", then click "Save".
5. Wait 1–2 minutes. Your app is now live at:
   https://YOUR-GITHUB-NAME.github.io/sinhala-trainer/
   Your privacy policy is at:
   https://YOUR-GITHUB-NAME.github.io/sinhala-trainer/privacy.html
6. Open the first link on your phone to check the app works.


STEP 2 — MAKE THE ANDROID APP FILE (free, with PWABuilder)
1. Go to pwabuilder.com
2. Paste your app link from Step 1 and click "Start".
3. Click "Package for stores" > "Android" > "Generate package".
   Package ID suggestion:  io.github.YOURGITHUBNAME.sinhalatrainer
4. Download the zip. Inside you will find:
   - an .aab file  (this is what you upload to Google Play)
   - a signing key file and a text file with its passwords.
   KEEP THE KEY FILE AND PASSWORDS SAFE. You need them for every future update.
5. The zip also contains "assetlinks.json". Create a folder named
   ".well-known" in your GitHub repository and upload assetlinks.json into it.
   (PWABuilder's own instructions in the zip explain this. It removes the
   address bar from the app.)


STEP 3 — CREATE YOUR GOOGLE PLAY DEVELOPER ACCOUNT
1. Go to play.google.com/console and sign in.
2. Choose "Personal" account (for yourself).
3. Pay the one-time US$25 fee and complete identity verification.


STEP 4 — CREATE THE APP IN PLAY CONSOLE
1. Click "Create app". Name: Sinhala Word Trainer. Type: App. Free.
2. Fill in "Set up your app": privacy policy link (from Step 1),
   app category = Education, content rating questionnaire, target audience
   (choose 18+ or 13+ to keep it simple), data safety form
   (answer: the app collects NO data).
3. Store listing — copy the texts below.
4. Upload graphics: app icon = icons/icon-512.png.
   Screenshots: take 2–4 screenshots of the app on your phone.
   Feature graphic (1024 x 500) — ask Claude to make one if you need it.


STEP 5 — CLOSED TESTING (required for new personal accounts)
1. In Play Console go to "Testing" > "Closed testing" > create a track.
2. Upload the .aab file from Step 2.
3. Add at least 12 testers (their Gmail addresses) — e.g. course-mates.
   They must join and keep the app installed for 14 days.
4. After 14 days, click "Apply for production" and answer Google's questions.
5. When Google approves, publish to "Production". Anyone can now install it.


UPDATING THE APP LATER (e.g. adding Lesson 2)
- Upload the new index.html to GitHub. Because the Play Store app opens your
  website, users get the update automatically — no new Play upload needed.
- In sw.js change "sinhala-trainer-v1" to "v2" so phones fetch the new version.


STORE LISTING TEXTS (copy and paste)

App name:
Sinhala Word Trainer

Short description (max 80 characters):
Learn confusing Sinhala words with Hindi & English meanings, flashcards, quiz.

Full description:
Sinhala Word Trainer helps Hindi-speaking learners master the Sinhala words
that are most often confused.

Hindi uses one word where Sinhala often uses two or three — है, देखना, सुनना,
नहीं. This app explains each difference side by side, with Hindi as your
learning bridge.

Lesson 1 covers five key groups:
• ඉන්නවා / තියෙනවා / තියනවා — to be (living), to be (things), to put
• බලනවා / පේනවා / දකිනවා / පෙන්නනවා — look, be visible, see, show
• අහනවා / ඇහෙනවා — ask & listen, be heard
• කියනවා / කියවනවා / කියලා — say, read, "that"
• නැහැ / නෙමෙයි / බැහැ / එපා — the four kinds of "not"

Features:
• Clear rules, Hindi bridge notes, memory tricks and common-mistake warnings
• Example sentences in Sinhala, Roman letters, Hindi and English
• Literary (written) and colloquial (spoken) forms clearly marked
• Flashcards with progress tracking
• Quiz with explanations and review of mistakes
• Searchable word list
• Works offline, no sign-in, no ads, no data collected
