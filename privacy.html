Privacy Policy — Sinhala Word Trainer
Developer: Khem Chetry
Last updated: 16 November 2026
SummarySinhala Word Trainer does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information.
Information we collectNone. The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking.
Data stored on your deviceYour flashcard progress ("I knew it" marks) and your best quiz score are saved only in your phone's browser storage, so you can continue where you left off. This data never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.
Third-party servicesThe app loads its fonts from Google Fonts. Google may receive your device's IP address when the fonts are downloaded, as described in Google's privacy policy. No other third-party services are used.
ChildrenThe app is an educational tool and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
ChangesIf this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
